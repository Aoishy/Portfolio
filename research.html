Research

Selected work in machine learning, security, and graph theory.

GPT-2: Transformer-based Text Generation and Spam Classification | Python, PyTorch, HuggingFace Transformers

  • Implemented the GPT-2 architecture from first principles, including masked multi-head self-attention and autoregressive language modeling.
  • Pretrained on a 50MB book corpus to learn contextual language representations for coherent long-form text generation.
  • Leveraged pretrained GPT-2 weights to improve convergence stability and semantic generalization.
  • Converted the generative model into a discriminative framework via a task-specific classification head; fine-tuned on SMS Spam dataset achieving 97%+ accuracy.
  • Performed controlled experiments comparing training-from-scratch vs. transfer learning to evaluate efficiency and generalization performance.

Extension of Explainable AMR Prediction Pipeline in Pseudomonas aeruginosa | Python, Scikit-learn, SHAP

  • Reproduced and extended a published two-tier machine learning pipeline for antimicrobial resistance (AMR) prediction from whole-genome sequences.
  • Implemented k-mer feature extraction with χ2-based feature selection and stratified train-test validation.
  • Enhanced Tier-I (Resistant vs Susceptible) prediction using ensemble methods (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting), improving robustness over baseline Decision Tree (79% accuracy, AUC 0.77).
  • Conducted comparative performance analysis across models using Accuracy, MCC, Sensitivity, Specificity, and AUC-ROC.
  • Applied SHAP-based interpretability to identify resistance-associated genomic signatures.

Algorithmic and Structural Study of Equitable Fair Domination in Graphs | Graph Theory, Algorithms | Ongoing

  • Designing efficient algorithms to compute equitable fair dominating sets and the equitable fair domination number γefd(G).
  • Establishing theoretical results for special graph classes, including trees, cycles, and complete bipartite graphs.
  • Exploring connections with classical domination, equitable domination, and fair domination frameworks.
  • Preparing results for academic publication in theoretical computer science and discrete mathematics.
